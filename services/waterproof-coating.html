What is included in our waterproof coating service

Our waterproof coating package is designed for Surat weather and local property styles. We begin with site verification and discuss the present wall condition, old coating health, moisture points, and required repairs. Based on this review, we suggest a practical scope that balances look, durability, and budget. The team protects furniture and flooring, completes sanding and putty work, and applies suitable primer before final coats. This professional sequence improves adhesion and color consistency while reducing patchiness.

Many clients ask how to maintain painted surfaces after project handover. We provide a simple checklist that covers cleaning intervals, ventilation tips, dampness prevention, and stain handling. For families with children or pets, we recommend low-odor and low-VOC paint options. For rental flats, we suggest economical shades that still look premium in photos. For owner-occupied homes, we plan long-term finishes with better washability and UV resistance. This approach makes waterproof coating a meaningful investment rather than a short-term cosmetic fix.
